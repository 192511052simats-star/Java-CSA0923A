TASK 12 ANALYSIS NOTES

Impact analysis:
- Two new vehicle types: e-bike and delivery van.
- New pricing rules: e-bike flat fee; delivery van double car rate from 09:00 inclusive
  to 17:00 exclusive.
- Visitors can obtain a one-day pass.
- Facility now has five floors with unequal capacities.
- A rectangular String[5][sameSlots] is unsuitable when floor capacities differ.
  A jagged String[][] allows each floor to have a different length.

Evaluation and decision:
Decision: MODIFY.
- Retain the Vehicle abstraction and existing Car concept.
- Add EBike and DeliveryVan subclasses.
- Add Pass interface and OneDayVisitorPass implementation.
- Add PricingRule abstraction with standard, e-bike-flat and delivery-van rules.
- Use jagged array for unequal floor capacities.
- Pricing rules are kept in separate strategy objects rather than scattered across
  all vehicle classes.
- Existing vehicle base abstraction remains unchanged; new categories and pricing
  strategies are added as extensions.

Evidence-based defence:
- The demo tests e-bike flat fee, delivery van peak and off-peak fees, visitor pass,
  five unequal floor capacities, and slot allocation.
- Future categories can be added with a Vehicle subclass and an appropriate PricingRule.
- Trade-off: more small classes/interfaces, but less conditional logic in one central
  method and better separation of pricing policy from vehicle identity.

Note: the demo treats the one-day pass as valid until the supplied LocalTime boundary.
In a production application, use LocalDateTime and a real pass issue/expiry date.
